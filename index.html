Good to know: organized activities need a permit

Leagues, practices, games, tournaments, camps and other organized programs at Hjelte Sports Center are scheduled through a City of Los Angeles Recreation and Parks permit. Casual recreation — a pickup game, a family afternoon, a walk around the field — is always welcome without one.

About Hjelte

One Complex.
Many Communities.

Hjelte Sports Center is a multi-use athletic facility in the Sepulveda Basin supporting organized sports, leagues, youth programs, independent athletes, informal groups, recreation and community activities — all sharing the same fields, paths and open turf.

Join the Hjelte Community

Hjelte belongs to everyone who uses it — permitted organizations, community and independent groups, and neighbours who just come to play. Joining is how the people who use the park share the work and the cost of keeping it good.

    No payment details are collected here. Joining records your name, email and phone with the hub so we can reach you — nothing more.

    2 Contribute

    Chip in when you're ready

    Pick the project and the group below, then choose your amount on Stripe's own secure page — card details never touch this site. Anyone can contribute for any group at Hjelte; a permit is not required. Both choices travel with the payment as its memo.

    Hjelte community members administer community contributions for Hjelte projects and upkeep. A 5% share of each contribution covers administration and processing; the rest goes to the fields. Contributions are not payments to the City of Los Angeles and do not grant permit status or exclusive use of any field.

    Who Keeps Hjelte Going

    The City of Los Angeles Department of Recreation and Parks maintains Hjelte Sports Center — the fields, the buildings and the grounds. Permitted upkeep and maintenance of the cricket pitch and its playing area is carried out by Los Angeles Cricket. Everything below is the volunteer effort logged on top of that, by the groups who play here.

    City of Los Angeles
    Recreation and Parks

    Maintains the park: mowing and irrigation, the restroom building, lighting, the diamonds and the shared outfield, waste collection and the perimeter road. Permits for organized activity are issued here too.

    Permitted upkeep by
    Los Angeles Cricket

    Maintains the cricket pitch and its playing area under permit: mowing, rolling and crease marking, boundary set-up on match days, and keeping the prepared turf playable between sessions.

      Getting a Field Permit at Hjelte

      Facility permits for softball, baseball, cricket and soccer use at Hjelte Sports Center are issued by the City of Los Angeles Department of Recreation and Parks, not by this community hub. Call the permit office on 818-765-0284 or see the official Hjelte Sports Center page for applications, fees and availability.
