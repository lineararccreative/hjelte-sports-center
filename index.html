About Hjelte

One Complex.
Many Communities.

Hjelte Sports Center is a multi-use athletic facility in the Sepulveda Basin supporting organized sports, leagues, youth programs, independent athletes, informal groups, recreation and community activities — all sharing the same fields, paths and open turf.
